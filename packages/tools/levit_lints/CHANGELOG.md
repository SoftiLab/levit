## 0.1.0

- Publish the native analyzer plugin with supported root-level configuration.
- Support Dart 3.10+ with analyzer 12.1 through 14.x.
- Add awaited lifecycle delegation, scoped widget lookup, and discarded known
  lifecycle/worker future diagnostics, with valid-pattern regression tests.
- Retain ownership, registration and reactive-status rules; remove obsolete
  ownership spellings and correct fixtures.
